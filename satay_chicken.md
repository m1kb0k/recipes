# Seasoned Chicken
## Seasoning Powder
* 1.5 tsp coriander
* 1.5 tsp cumin powder
* 1.5 tsp tumeric
* 1.5 tsp paprika
* 1.25 tsp chilli powder , adjust to taste (not US "Chili Powder", Note 1)
* 3.5 tsp curry powder , not HOT (any, Malaysian, generic, Clives of India, Keens)
* 1.25 tsp salt
* 2 tsp white sugar
## Other
* 750g Chicken
* Half an onion (grated)

1. Dice the chicken
2. Combine all spices to make the seasoning powder
3. Mix the chicken, onion and 3.5 TABLESPOONS of seasoning powder in a bowl
4. let sit to marinate for at least 20 mins, preferably 3 hours, overnite is better
5. In batches, brown the chicken in a non stick pan (outside cooked but inside raw). Keep it warm and covered in a bowl

# Satay Sauce
* 2 tbsp oil
* 1/4 cup finely diced onion
* 4 minced garlic cloves
* 1 cup chicken broth / stock
* 3 TABLEspoons peanut butter
* 2 tsp soy sauce
* 3 tsp soy sauce
* 400g coconut milk (preferably full fat but light will be ok)
* 2 tbsp peanut butter


1. In a pot, on medium heat, add 1 tablespoon of oil, onion, and garlic. Saute until onion is transluscent - about 2 mins
2. Add the rest of the seasonin powder an cook for a minute
3. Add chicken stock and 2 TABLESPOONS of peanut butter
4. Add 3 TABLESPOONS of peanut butter 5 tsp of soy sauce, coconut milk
5. Simmer for 15 mins until thickened
